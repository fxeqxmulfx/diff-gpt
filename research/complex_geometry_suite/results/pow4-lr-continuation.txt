Fourth-power seed 1: matched learning-rate continuation

Reducing LR from 0.002 to 0.0002 strongly reduced held-out accuracy oscillation
in this run. It did not achieve 99% or materially improve the fixed endpoint:
97.72582359% versus97.76833156%, a difference of two held-out examples.
This supports optimizer-step sensitivity of the instability while leaving a
generalization gap. It does not identify a single responsible parameter block.

Design fixed before training
  Parent: complex-hermitian/arithmetic-sum-pow4/seed1, 100000 completed updates.
  Identical weights, both AdamW moments, global counter and next sample stream.
  Each arm: 19000 more updates, 4704000 examples, 1000 additional epochs.
  Full 4704 training and 4705 held-out examples scored every 19 updates.
  No moment reset, warmup restart, architecture change or early stopping.
  AdamW betas (.9, .98), epsilon 1e-8 and decay coefficient .1 are unchanged.
  Since AdamW decay scales with LR, this changes both the adaptive displacement
  and per-step shrinkage. It does not isolate their separate contributions.

Final 200 means observations at additional epochs 801 through 1000; all 1001
observations, including initialization, are retained. Largest drop is measured
over the complete continuation. Standard deviation is descriptive, not a
confidence interval; consecutive observations are correlated. The lower LR
reduces the final 200 standard deviation by 68.415x and largest adjacent drop
by 44.182x. Its mean is 5.3360 percentage points higher, but endpoint loss is
higher and neither arm reaches 99%. Do not summarize this as solving the task.
The recorded final training loss is 4.8587922e-6 versus 0.0 in the native FP32
evaluation; recorded zero does not imply exact mathematical zero loss.

Relation to the weight diagnosis
  The starting checkpoint had finite, structurally valid weights and moments,
  with no collapsed Q/K heads or near-zero RMS denominator blow-up.
  Its relative saved-moment-plus-decay step estimates were largest in Q/K and
  the FFN projections, around 0.6% of weight norm per update (Q 0.6644%, K 0.6417%,
  FFN gate 0.6483%, value 0.6508%, output 0.6171%). These describe historical moments,
  not a fresh minibatch update. They do not prove which block causes a drop.
  The LR intervention changes all parameter blocks together. The evidence
  supports sensitivity to optimizer step size, without localizing the failure
  or establishing that a particular normalizer or model block must be removed.

Integrity and recovery
  Both arms completed and passed artifact audits: 1001 observations each,
  exact matched exposure, shared sample hash, unchanged parent checkpoint,
  finite structurally valid parameter/moment states, and retained snapshots
  at additional updates 0, 9500, 19000 (epochs 0, 500, 1000).
  First-step gradients and updated moments match exactly. Actual displacement
  norm ratio is 0.09999998204, matching the prescribed 0.1 within FP32 rounding.
  First-step maximum scaling error is 6.55651093e-8.

  The original control completed before a reporting audit rejected decimal
  roundoff in derived summaries (maximum 1.11e-16). The corrected audit keeps
  counters and hashes exact and permits 1e-12 relative/absolute float tolerance.
  No completed control training was repeated. One reference update from the
  identical parent and first continuation batch was regenerated and discarded
  solely to check first-step equivalence with the lower-LR arm. This engineering
  work is recorded in audit-recovery.json, outside the two scientific budgets.
  Both runner versions are archived; numerical training code is text-identical
  and the canonical PTX hash matches the original trained model.
  Seven CPU tests, formatting and Clippy checks passed. No Python was executed.

Scope
  One selected failed seed, continued after its frozen training budget. This
  is a diagnostic, not a fresh-seed result, a general convergence guarantee,
  or a change to the original benchmark. The Hermitian suite remains 29/30 long
  and 75/75 short. The fixed plan forbids further LR arms or budget expansion
  based on these results. The earlier capacity queue remains stopped.
