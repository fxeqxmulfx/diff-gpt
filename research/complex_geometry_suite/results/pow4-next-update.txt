Direction diagnosis
For the ordinary next update, full-training gradient dot actual displacement
is +.0018543917. All eight parameter blocks contribute a positive term. Its
negative-displacement/full-gradient cosine is -.0822408, so the direction is
locally uphill even before considering finite-step curvature. This explains
why merely reducing the LR does not turn this particular step into descent.

diagnosis based only on historical saved moments.
