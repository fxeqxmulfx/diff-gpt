Complex geometry transformer, native Rust/Kernite

Build a separate candidate while the existing complex_ffn_suite trains.
Keep the radial-gated product sigma(sqrt(|g|^2+f32::EPSILON))*g*u/sqrt(2).
Four arms use identical real width, hidden width, head count and depth:
  real-product: unrestricted real projections, real XSA (fresh control).
  complex-linear: complex-linear projections, real XSA (projection ablation).
  complex-hermitian: complex-linear projections, Hermitian XSA (candidate).
  complex-hermitian-phase: same candidate with paired-variance AdamW.

Complex vectors use half-split real/imaginary coordinates within each head.
FFN gate and value each use one half-split complex vector. Every learned
projection is expanded from A+iB as [[A,-B],[B,A]] in the appropriate layout.
Embedding entries are free complex coordinates. Readout is Re<E_y,x>.
RMS retains the original real-coordinate scale: sqrt(eps+sum |z|^2/(2m)).
Q/K RMS, complex RoPE, real Hermitian attention scores, SSMax log(N)/D,
and 1/sqrt(2*layers) residual scaling after each branch RMS are unchanged.
Hermitian XSA is a-v*<v,a>/max(||v||,1e-12)^2, zero on the first query;
the constant-prefix centering/guard is retained with the same clipped norm.
Above the clip it removes the entire complex line spanned by v, including iv.

Ordinary coordinate AdamW for the first three arms. The fourth uses one second
moment per complex pair, driven by (g_real^2+g_imag^2)/2; first moments remain
complex. This is covariant under rotations of each parameter's complex axes.
It does not assert invariance under arbitrary mixing of channels or convergence.
All arms use betas(.9,.98), epsilon1e-8, decay.1, warmup50,
LR twice the frozen task rate. Contract expanded gradients BEFORE moments,
then reconstruct exact block constraints. Complex arms share initialization.
All arms share the frozen sampler. Fixed dimensions are NOT parameter matched:
complex projection matrices have half as many independent real parameters.
Record independent and expanded counts. No learning-rate or hidden-width tuning.

All 35 frozen tasks, data hashes, budgets and observation cadence are retained;
seeds0/1/2, 25 short then10 long, 420 cells if the full cohort is requested.
Long tasks are diagnostic, not a tuning set. One native sequence engine handles
both arithmetic and longer sequences. Every miss/error remains in reports.
Save buffered observations and parameters/moments every500 complete epochs,
plus initialization, first99 crossing, budget end or clean stop. Never save
on every epoch. Abrupt execution windows require investigation, not silent replay.
No competing full cohort is launched as part of this build request.

This is an experimental architecture, not a theorem of optimality or convergence.
The quadratic product changes global phase by twice the angle; residual addition
therefore does NOT make the whole model globally phase-equivariant. RMS, real
scores and radial gates are nonholomorphic, real-differentiable complex operations.
The flat-spectrum Lean bridge is conditional and does not establish this
attention rule or guarantee that AdamW learns any specific modular task.
