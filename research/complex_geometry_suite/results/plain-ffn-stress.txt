Plain complex-product FFN preflight, 2026-11-08

The final preflight passes 40 plain-model cases, three radial regression cases,
32 product rows and six local adjoint checks. There are 1438 plain-model and
96 radial-model parameter-direction checks. The isolated product checks cover
11584 input-gradient coordinates. No optimizer updates occur in this audit.

Candidate: replace the existing radial-gated complex product with g*u/sqrt(2).
The new Kernite kernels keep the half-split complex layout and use explicit
real cross terms. The independent reference uses scalar FP64 forward-mode AD.
Every supported complex projection, normalizer, residual and tied readout is
included in full-model probes. Frozen native kernels are not edited.

Coverage
Primitive complex widths 1/3/17/341, eight rows each, include zero gate with
nonzero value, scales1e-10 through1e8, and mixed1e20/1e-20 inputs. Guarded
overlaunch tails and unchanged inputs are checked. Maximum forward relative
error is1.119e-7 and gradient relative error8.476e-8, below the5e-6 bounds.

Full-model cases include widths64/128/256, depths1/2/4, lengths1--128,
masked labels, repeated prefixes, tiny/large embeddings and Q/K, zero/clipped/
small values, tiny/large products, zero projection outputs and confident logits.
Every one of the12 retained corrected radial checkpoints is probed with the
plain FFN on three fixed examples. These are saved-weight derivative probes,
not claims that plain inference preserves radial predictions.

Each independent parameter block receives two dense directions, its largest
gradient coordinate, and its gradient direction when nonzero. The unchanged
full-model bounds are5e-5 of logit scale,5e-5 relative loss, and5e-4 times the
native block-gradient norm plus1e-12 for each directional derivative. The
largest final directional error consumes only.017271 of its allowed bound.
Finite stress coverage does not prove accuracy for every possible tensor.

Preserved failure and correction
plain-ffn-stress-01 passes all32 product rows and33/34 plain-model cases. Its
single128-wide-head case fails Q/K gradient-aligned directions: errors4.916e-8
and5.025e-8, roughly.24% of their small gradients, versus the.05% bound.
The standalone plain product, Peri-RMS and XSA local adjoints all pass.
The discrepancy arises in the composed, nearly scale-invariant path, where
FP32 intermediate rounding contaminates an epsilon-scale radial derivative.

Recomputing only output projection -> RMS is insufficient. That unsuccessful
kernel and trace are retained as plain-ffn-chain-attempt-01.rs and
plain-ffn-head128-trace-02.json. The final stable_chain.rs recomputes the entire
XSA -> output projection -> RMS Q/K adjoint in FP64, including the clipped
projection term, before storing Q/K-related score derivatives. It leaves
the ordinary forward, V adjoint and projection-parameter adjoint unchanged.
The composed source formula is unchanged. The precision dispatch applies
when stable attention is enabled for a single128-wide head.

The independent full-path Q direction discrepancy falls from.0023687 relative
to1.5164e-7. Local Q/K comparisons must differentiate the new composed map
against its normalized-branch upstream gradient: comparing it to the old XSA
alone with the rounded intermediate adjoint is a different derivative. That
obsolete comparison remains visible in trace-03; the final independently
defined composed reference passes in plain-ffn-stress-02/wide-composed-adjoint.json.
The other local checks remain the plain product, Peri-RMS, output projection
and XSA V. All six pass. The successful suite adds six wide-head cases for
depth, masks/tail, real XSA and zero/clipped/small values, plus a radial
wide-head regression. No failing case was removed or bound relaxed.

Effect on the training comparison
Both seed1 training models have four32-wide heads. The new precision dispatch
is inactive there; the only change from the corrected radial control is the
FFN gate removal. The protocol explicitly records training_uses_wide_chain=false.
The complete current source fingerprint is checked against the successful
preflight before training can start. Original and corrected control files
remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The existing corrected-radial endpoint replay
also passes under the default mode.
