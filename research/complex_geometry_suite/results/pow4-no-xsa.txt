What improved and what remains open
The unchanged first99 criterion is now met on this previously failing seed.
The plain FFN, Peri/embedding norms, residual scale and tied weights are retained.
The controlled operator change and restored gradient paths support investigating
XSA as a limitation on this task. They do not establish optimality of ordinary
attention or the exact learned arithmetic representation.

Oscillations remain before the crossing. The ordinary last200 mean is slightly
LOWER than the matched control mean, despite a higher best observation and
lower standard deviation. Its worst single drop still exceeds22 points. Its
trajectory after epoch2648 is unobserved: do not extrapolate sustained99% or
late-run stability from the stopped run. The threshold is crossed by the
minimum integer count4658; no accuracy margin or new held-out split is claimed.
This is one known-miss task/seed diagnostic, not universal-model promotion.
The frozen short-selection/long-regression checks are still needed before
choosing this as the universal replacement. The capacity queue stays stopped.

Matched protocol
The control is runs/complex-pow4-plain-ffn-seed1-01 at repository root. Both
models start with exactly the original seed1 weights and zero optimizer state,
use the same complete100000-update sampler,4704/4705 split,batch256/tail96,
width128,heads4,depth1,hidden342,and110848 independent/209280 expanded parameters.
AdamW LR.002,betas(.9,.98),epsilon1e-8,decay.1,warmup50 are unchanged, including
the first zero-LR update's moment insertion. No parameter,initializer,FFN,
learning-rate,depth,width or seed search was added.

Attention keeps QK RMS,RoPE and the original SSMax log(N)/head_width probability
calculation. The ordinary value sum replaces the Hermitian projection output;
XSA-only first/repeated attention suppression and its adjoint masks are disabled.
The unused frozen XSA result is overwritten after reusing its probability
calculation. This preserves score arithmetic, at the cost of some redundant
work; runtime optimization was not part of this experiment. The plain complex
product,parameter-free Peri/embedding RMS,tied readout and post-RMS residual
scaling1/sqrt(2L) remain. Both variant tags and kernel hashes govern replay.
