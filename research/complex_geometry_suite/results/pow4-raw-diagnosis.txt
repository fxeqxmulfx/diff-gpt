Raw-weight diagnosis: fourth-power arithmetic, Hermitian seed 1
2026-11-08

Findings

The saved weights do not show an explosion or corruption. At a weak saved
checkpoint the optimizer's historical direction is locally uphill across all
eight parameter blocks, and the largest relative step scales are in the FFN.
A separate, concrete loss/gradient precision defect appears after the lower-LR
run becomes highly confident. These findings must not be conflated: the
precision defect is large at the low-LR endpoint and negligible at the saved
weak checkpoint. Its role in the original accuracy crashes remains unproven.

What was read and tested

Directly decoded little-endian FP32 parameters and both AdamW moment arrays
from all six continuation checkpoints: both LRs at additional epochs 0, 500,
1000. Each file has 209280 expanded weights and two equally sized moment arrays;
the complex model has 110848 independent real parameters. Both initial states
are bit-identical to the original exhausted checkpoint. Also read the successful
Hermitian seed 0 and seed 2 endpoints as descriptive references, not age-matched
controls or coordinates suitable for transplanting into seed 1.

Hashes, finite values and complex ties pass. Six native full-split replays
reproduce saved exact counts and losses, with parameters and moments unchanged.
Then evaluated 26 in-memory block substitutions and 32 local perturbations.
All copies were discarded; no training update or input checkpoint write occurred.
An additional 12 split comparisons checked native loss/gradients against a
FP64 reference applied to the exact same native FP32 logits.

1. What the raw weights and moments show

At +500 epochs, original LR has 91.47715% held-out accuracy and lower LR has
98.02338%. Original-LR block statistics are:

  Block                 Weight RMS    Historical step / weight norm
  embedding/readout     0.135521      0.17312%
  Q                     0.074169      0.32124%
  K                     0.074387      0.33727%
  V                     0.075284      0.33560%
  attention output      0.077994      0.34035%
  FFN gate              0.062806      0.41386%
  FFN value             0.062918      0.40395%
  FFN output            0.066362      0.37560%

Historical step means LR*(m/(sqrt(v)+1e-8)+decay*w), evaluated using stored
moments. Bias corrections are effectively one at these global steps. This is
not the next actual minibatch update, which would first change both moments.

All eight historical directions have more than 99.97% of their squared norm
perpendicular to the block weight vector: substantial directional motion, not
just radial shrinkage. At the weak checkpoint the largest absolute independent
weight is embedding[flat 2927] = -0.80385667. Q/K maxima are about 0.33; FFN maxima
about 0.34--0.36. Across all six snapshots the maximum absolute weight is
1.04615462 in the lower-LR embedding. Exact coordinates and raw values for the
largest weights and momentum steps are retained in raw_blocks in weights.json.

The weak checkpoint's projection norms are smaller than the parent, not larger.
Its Q/K singular-energy participation is 23.65/23.87 and FFN gate/value about
75.61/74.59. These are continuous spectral concentration statistics, not literal
matrix ranks. No abrupt spectral collapse is visible at this saved weak state.

2. Direct local checks of the optimizer direction

For each block separately, move a temporary copy by 0.1% of that block's weight
norm along the stored optimizer descent direction, preserving complex ties.
At the weak +500 checkpoint, every one of the eight moves increases complete
training cross entropy; all eight reverse moves decrease it. Example:

  Baseline training CE                  0.00406217149
  Embedding move in stored direction    0.00418235443
  Embedding reverse move                0.00395236311
  Attention-output stored direction     0.00410544943
  Attention-output reverse direction    0.00402053076

This strengthens the diagnosis of stale/misaligned momentum at that checkpoint.
It does not prove that the actual next optimizer step will be uphill, nor
identify the historical crash trigger between sparse saved weight states.
The local changes alter held-out accuracy by at most two of 4705 examples.

Transplanting only Q+K from the steadier +500 model changes the weak model from
91.47715% to 91.51966%: two examples. No individual block transplant repairs it.
Several hybrids degrade strongly in both directions (embedding or attention
output especially), consistent with co-adapted representations. Their failure
is not evidence that the transplanted block is inherently defective. Copying
all eight blocks reproduces the donor's exact complete-split scores in both
directions, validating the transplant mechanism.

3. Concrete numerical defect at high training confidence

The native loss kernel in native/src/sequence_ops.rs accumulates softmax mass
in FP32, computes the target derivative as p_target-1 (line 363), and evaluates
loss as log(total)+max-target_logit (lines 372--375). Near probability one,
these operations lose the small corrective signal through rounding and
cancellation. The defect is not limited to the displayed loss value.

The diagnostic computes softmax on identical FP32 logits promoted to FP64,
sets the target derivative to -sum(non-target probabilities), and casts those
derivatives to FP32 before using the unchanged model backward kernels.
Both backwards leave weights and moments untouched.

  Lower LR at +1000 epochs:
    Native mean training loss              0
    Reference mean training loss           9.64340502e-8
    Native zero loss / positive reference  4704/4704 examples
    Lost nonzero target-logit derivatives  3628/4704 examples
    Logit-gradient relative L2 error       61.87594%

Concrete training input (51,60), target 4: native target derivative is 0;
the reference is -1.03164458e-7. Native other-class derivatives still sum to
+1.03164475e-7. Cross-entropy logit gradients should sum to zero because adding
a common constant to all logits leaves the loss unchanged. The reference
preserves that identity; the native computation loses the negative term here.
These are unaveraged per-example derivatives; batch scaling is undone for
this comparison.

The error propagates into parameter-gradient direction. At the low-LR endpoint,
cosines between native and corrected full-training gradients are:

  embedding/readout  +0.050
  Q                  -0.086
  K                  -0.132
  V                  +0.331
  attention output   +0.021
  FFN gate           -0.137
  FFN value          -0.071
  FFN output         -0.239

These are tiny gradients in absolute terms, but their direction matters once
AdamW's second moments are also tiny. At low-LR +500, every Q/K coordinate has
sqrt(v) below AdamW epsilon 1e-8; the median is about 3.4--3.7e-10. At the weak
original-LR checkpoint the median is about 3.5e-4 and the gradient reference
disagrees by only roughly 2e-6 in relative parameter-gradient norm. Thus the
precision defect is confirmed in the saturated low-LR regime, not established
as the cause of the original weak state or previous accuracy crashes.

Held-out exact classification counts are unchanged by this gradient-only
diagnostic. Existing accuracy misses are real, not a loss-reporting artifact.

4. Why the steadier run still misses 99%

The lower-LR endpoint classifies every training example correctly with median
target-versus-competitor logit margin 18.36, but still misses 105 held-out inputs.
Only 7 of 42 held-out repeated-token inputs are correct, versus 55/55 training
repeated-token inputs. These 35 failures are one third of the held-out errors;
70 other errors remain. Mean margin on wrong held-out examples is -5.81.
This is incomplete generalization with confident errors, not just jitter around
the classification threshold.

Embedding/readout Frobenius norm grows from 15.60 to 21.30 in the lower-LR
continuation while Q/K norms shrink to about 68.38% of their starting values.
Readout concentration changes, but the available evidence does not justify
removing tied embeddings. Residual cancellation increases, yet saved final
residual RMS stays at least 0.2554; no near-zero final normalization divisor
is present on these held-out examples. Peri-RMS and embedding RMS are not
disproved by these measurements.

Action suggested by the diagnosis

Before changing the architecture, make a separate corrected loss-kernel
candidate that retains the small target gradient and avoids cancellation in
cross entropy. Test the high-confidence regime explicitly, then compare matched
training runs if authorized. Changing only reported loss is insufficient.
Keep the frozen native backend, checkpoints and 29/30 benchmark outcome intact.
The correction is not yet a proven convergence fix; no corrected-loss training
was run in this investigation. Do not infer that a bigger model or another
normalizer is required from the current evidence.
