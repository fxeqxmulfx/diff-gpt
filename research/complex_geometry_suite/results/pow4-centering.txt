Scope and interpretation
This is a single known-miss task,(a+b)^4 mod97,seed1. The change is pure causal
prefix centering instead of ordinary attention; no XSA projection is added.
The plain complex product, Peri/embedding RMSNorm, QK normalization, SSMax
log(N)/head_width, Q/K-only RoPE, residual scaling after RMS and tied weights
are unchanged. Dimensions128wide/4heads/1layer, initialization, zero moments,
splits, sample order, AdamW and100000-update maximum match the frozen plan.
No new seeds, optimizer arms, capacity experiments or short/long suite ran.

The source paper is https://arxiv.org/html/2410.07799v3, Sections2.4 and3.2.
Its correction is global rank-one; ours uses causal prefix means. Its random-
matrix initialization theory is not a convergence guarantee for this model.
Section4 excludes training dynamics. This result does not refute the paper.

The Lean check proves constant-value cancellation, zero shared-parameter
attention derivatives, residual preservation, and impossibility ONLY for a
decoder that sees the canceled signal alone. It does not prove the full
transformer cannot solve the task. The residual/FFN still carry information.
Our numerical checks reproduce the diagonal attention cancellation exactly.
That mechanism is real; it is not established as the sole cause of the much
broader observed generalization failure. Keep ordinary attention as the
incumbent for this comparison; do not promote pure centering.
