Implementation
ordinary_attention.rs reuses the original causal SSMax probability calculation
and overwrites its unused XSA output with the ordinary weighted value sum. Its
anchored evaluation v_first + sum_j p_j*(v_j-v_first) preserves equal values.
The original probability arithmetic, QK RMS, RoPE and masking are unchanged;
probabilities naturally change when model states/weights change during training.
The ordinary Q/score adjoint uses FP64 dot products and avoids an intermediate
FP32 dP cast. The existing deterministic K/V accumulation is retained with
unprojected upstream gradients and zero exclusion correction. There are no
atomic reductions or new trainable parameters.

Engine::enable_ordinary_attention initializes zero prefix flags. The forward
does not replace those flags with XSA's guard. The attention residual includes
first/repeated positions, and its backward uses ordinary scaling without the
XSA-only mask. Zero flags make FFN/output sharing the identity. The default XSA
mode is unchanged. Checkpoint replay selects attention explicitly and validates
the ordinary PTX hash. The isolated oracle sums ordinary values directly in
FP64, while its complete model retains every token path without XSA shortcuts.
