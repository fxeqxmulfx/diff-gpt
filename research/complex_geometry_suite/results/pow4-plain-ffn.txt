a=b are16/42 (38.095238%); off-diagonal examples are4633/4663 (99.356637%).

Controlled change
The old FFN computes sigmoid(sqrt(|g|^2+eps))*g*u/sqrt(2); the candidate computes
g*u/sqrt(2). The same complex-linear matrices,110848 independent parameters
(209280 expanded), width128, heads4, depth1 and hidden342 are retained. Both
use Peri/embedding parameter-free RMS, tied embeddings, Hermitian XSA, QK RMS,
SSMax inverse-width gain, RoPE and post-RMS residual scaling1/sqrt(2L).
Initialization, complete sampler,4704/4705 split, batch256/tail96, AdamW LR.002,
betas(.9,.98), epsilon1e-8, decay.1 and50-update warmup are matched. The first
zero-LR update inserts moments without changing weights. No extra seed, width,
depth, LR, initializer or optimizer was tried. The separate capacity queue stays
stopped. This known-miss diagnostic does not replace frozen benchmark selection.
