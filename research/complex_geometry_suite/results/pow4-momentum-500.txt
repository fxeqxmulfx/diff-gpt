Matched 500-epoch first-moment reset: (a+b)^4 mod 97, seed 1
2026-11-08

Resetting AdamW's first moment once does not fix convergence in this window.
Neither branch reaches 99%. The unchanged branch has higher best, final and
final-200 mean held-out accuracy. Its entire trajectory reproduces the original
run, including a byte-identical epoch-4500 parameter/moment state.

Protocol
Both branches restore the corrected-gradient epoch-4000 checkpoint, completed
76000 updates. Each then performs exactly 9500 updates / 500 epochs, ending at
global step 85500. Total actual training work: 19000 updates. Both use the same
2352000 next training examples, LR .002, betas (.9,.98), epsilon 1e-8, decay .1,
Adam clock, numerical kernels and model. The reset branch zeroes only m ONCE.
It retains v and does not restart warmup or adjust LR. Peri/embedding RMS,
residual connections/scaling, complex radial product FFN and tied weights stay.
The two branches are fixed before observing results; no additional arms run.

All 501 observations include complete train and held-out scores, separately
counting diagonal a=b and non-diagonal examples. First99 is recorded, but the
fixed diagnostic window would continue after a crossing to measure persistence.
There were no crossings or stops. History is buffered; full p/m/v snapshots
are saved only at the initial state and after 500 epochs. No per-epoch writes.

The diagonal gap persists
The unchanged endpoint fits all 55 training diagonals but only 10/42 held-out
diagonals. Its non-diagonal held-out score is 4609/4663 = 98.841947%.
Thus 32 of its 86 errors are diagonal. Correcting all 32 alone would still
leave 54 errors, whereas 99% allows at most 47 errors out of 4705.
The reset endpoint scores 55/55 training diagonals and 15/42 held-out diagonals,
but its non-diagonal score falls to 4239/4663 = 90.907141%.
At each branch's best observation, training is 4704/4704, while held-out
diagonals are only 14/42 (keep) and 13/42 (reset). This is a generalization gap,
not simply a failure to fit the observed examples.

Interpretation
The previous one-step experiment correctly established a locally harmful
stored first-moment direction at epoch4000. The present first updates reproduce
those exact saved p/m/v bytes. However, ordinary AdamW rebuilds momentum after
the single reset, and the local improvement does not translate into a better
500-epoch trajectory. Do not turn that local diagnosis into a universal cause
of the 99% miss. This experiment neither demonstrates a normalization/residual
defect nor establishes that the current FFN is optimal.

During this experiment the user requested rereading the Lean flat-spectrum
work. Its latest source bridge concerns a plain complex product, while this
transformer retains a radial gate. Removing only that gate is a reasonable
next FFN ablation, not a proven remedy. The related theorem has explicit
feature-recovery/alignment/margin premises and does not prove AdamW convergence.
The quartic task also admits a rank-four scalar label signal; availability
does not guarantee its discovery or a sufficient learned classifier. See
pow4-flat-spectrum-ffn-review.txt for the source comparison and native check.
No additional FFN training or new architecture was run in this experiment.
