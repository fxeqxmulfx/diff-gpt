The native data compatibility code implements the SeedSequence and PCG64
algorithms described by the NumPy source. No NumPy or Python runtime is used.

https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.2.0/numpy/random/bit_generator.pyx
https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.2.0/numpy/random/src/pcg64/pcg64.h
https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.2.0/numpy/random/src/pcg64/pcg64.c
https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.2.0/numpy/random/src/distributions/distributions.c
