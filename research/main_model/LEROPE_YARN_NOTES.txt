Source facts: LeRoPE section3 uses zero-initialized log multipliers shared
across all heads/layers,without frequency weight decay and with separate
clipping. AppendixD.2 interpolates each model's learned frequencies and applies
YaRN's logit temperature at inference. No extension gives the identity.
Our selected main matches that LeRoPE parameterization; Log-Scaled LeRoPE's
previously tested p*ln(p+1) angle is a separate,rejected experiment.

Image source:https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13298v1,section3. Axial RoPE allocates
channel pairs separately to row/column coordinates; mixed RoPE combines both
coordinates within a pair. We propose the axial version for this queue item.
