All Adam coordinates use beta(.9,.99), epsilon1e-8 after the bias-corrected
variance square root. Global clipping at1 applies to independent physical
gradients before factor differentiation. Adam acts on real/imaginary coordinates
separately; phase-equivariant adaptive histories are not assumed. Every MD family
has softplus row/column gains initialized to1 and fixed direction radius equal
to the actual initial complex Frobenius norm. The tied embedding remains a single
unconstrained auxiliary group. This deviates from the paper's separately
normalized embedding/head groups and its initialization; all cells share it.

AdamMD: Appendix A Algorithm2 with ordinary bias-corrected Adam on the direction
and both raw gain vectors. Project the candidate direction to its fixed radius;
fuse using updated gains. No extra direction scaling and no weight decay.

AdamMDW: the local Lean composition Transformer.MDW.adamMDWProposal, not a named
optimizer in the paper. Compute the same AdamMD proposal, subtract
eta_direction*0.1*OLD physical W, then repair only storage. If Wnew is nonzero,
s=||Wnew/(new_row*new_col)||_F/radius; multiply every row gain by s and update
its raw inverse-softplus value. Keep physical Wnew, columns, and all optimizer
moments unchanged by repair. A zero corrected matrix keeps finite gains and
zero direction. Auxiliary embedding AdamW uses its own scheduled LR and decay0.1.
Complex Hermitian gain derivatives and complex norms extend the real Lean rule;
the experiment is not a Lean convergence theorem for complex training.

MuonMD: unchanged raw Nesterov momentum0.95, five NS iterations, complex adjoint
products, fixed multiplier radius/sqrt(min(complex_rows,complex_cols)); same
projection and raw-gain Adam as before. No per-update rescaling of the Muon step.

Schedules, zero-based update t=0..99999 and absolute per-group final LR1e-8:
- Warmup h=1000: rate=peak*t/1000 for t<1000; t=1000 uses peak. h=0 has no warmup.
  At rate0, update moment histories but preserve physical weights and gains.
- Linear after warmup: rate=peak+(1e-8-peak)*(t-h)/(99999-h).
- WSD: retain peak after warmup until t=80000. In the final20000 updates use
  u=(t-80000)/19999 and rate=peak+(1e-8-peak)*sqrt(u), i.e. a1-sqrt cooldown.
  At t=80000 the rate is peak; at t=99999 it is1e-8. All groups share the shape
  and warmup but have their own absolute peak and floor.
- The two extra MuonMD arms replace sqrt(u) by cbrt(u) or sqrt(sqrt(u)); the
  cooldown remains20% and warmup remains0. These are targeted figure controls,
  not additional levels of the complete four-optimizer/warmup factorial.
The1000-step warmup is the paper's absolute count; its dense budget was50000,
so this study's warmup fraction is1%, not its2%. Discrete endpoints are explicit
local choices. Existing linear/no-warmup rates must match bit for bit.
