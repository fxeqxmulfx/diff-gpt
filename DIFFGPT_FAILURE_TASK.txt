Established evidence
The additional DiffGPT study ran 36 cells: 3 encoders (MASH, LGT, MASH-half),
1 or 2 features, seeds 11/17/23, and 1D or axial 2D positions. Every cell
completed 5000 updates without reaching 90% decoded whole-row accuracy.
Passing implementation/provenance audits did not make these learning passes.
The selected main's 75 short and 30 long passes belong to a different suite.

The older architecture and its historical recipe passed a fresh Rust/Kernite
recheck on one-feature MASH-half Fibonacci mod97. Data, split, encoded examples,
decoder bases and all sampled training IDs match the corresponding failed
native DiffGPT cells by SHA256. Final checkpoints replay metrics exactly.

Seed  Old first >=90%, updates  Old final  Current 1D final  Current 2D final
11                      1300  94.982993%        81.972789%        82.142857%
17                      1500  93.239796%        88.839286%        89.328231%
23                      1150  92.346939%        77.380952%        76.679422%

All three older runs ended with 100% training accuracy. First90 is observed
every 50 updates; it is not a sustained-accuracy claim. These three passes
cover one-feature MASH-half, not the other encoder/two-feature cells.

Passing older baseline
- D128/H1/L1/V512; 262016 independent real parameters; SwiGLU hidden341.
- Unrestricted real projections, real centered XSA, standard 1D RoPE,
  attention scale 1/sqrt(128), residual gain 1, tied embedding/readout.
- Parameter-free Peri, embedding, final and per-head Q/K RMS normalization.
- ScheduleFreeAdamW: LR 0.03, betas (0.9,0.98), weight decay 1,
  epsilon 1e-8, warmup 10 updates, no clipping or cosine schedule.
- Training uses query weights y; evaluation and checkpoints use average
  weights x.
- Fixed FP32 GPU arithmetic. Output projection initialization uses
  0.02/sqrt(2L), as in the historical baseline.

Failing current baseline
- Same task geometry D128/H1/L1/V512, complex FFN hidden342; complex-linear
  projections, plain complex product, ordinary causal value aggregation,
  squared-imaginary score, four-stream phase-mHC and learned LeRoPE.
- SSMax score ln(N)/d * (Re(Z) + beta*Im(Z)^2/d), with Z=sum(conj(q)*k).
- Residual branch scale 1/sqrt(2L) after output RMS normalization.
- AdamW/WSD: peak LR 0.001, betas (0.9,0.95), weight decay 0.1,
  epsilon 1e-8, independent-coordinate clip 1, 2% warmup and final 20%
  cosine decay to 1e-8. Frequencies have separate clip 1 and weight decay 0.
- FP16 forward affine operands, TF32 backward affine operands; FP32
  accumulation, remaining model math, master weights and moments.

Candidate experiment: complex-pair AdamW
Try the existing Rust/Kernite implementation with shared second moment
v'=0.95*v+0.05*((g_re^2+g_im^2)/2) per complex pair. Keep componentwise first
moments, real/frequency groups and all other settings unchanged.
Compare on one-feature MASH-half Fibonacci mod97, axial 2D, seeds 11/17/23,
5000 updates, with matched data, sampler and initialization.
Report train/test decoded whole-row accuracy, losses, first90/first99 and final
metrics. Add native optimizer and checkpoint-continuation regression tests.
